# Publishing and safety checklist

An editor should complete this list for every edition.

## Rights and attribution

- [ ] We are permitted to process and publish summaries of this edition.
- [ ] The publication name, edition date, and official link are correct.
- [ ] No PDF page, photograph, illustration, or full article is exposed.
- [ ] Summaries use original wording and contain no unnecessary quotations.

## Accuracy

- [ ] Every page and every article/brief/caption was checked for medical,
      medicine, pharmacy, public-health, and healthcare content.
- [ ] All extraction warnings, low-confidence candidates, OCR gaps, and items
      rejected by validation were reviewed manually.
- [ ] The candidate safety ceiling was not reached; if it was, the affected
      source chunk was reviewed manually for omitted items.
- [ ] Medicine names, strengths, doses, routes, indications, contraindications,
      interactions, adverse effects, recalls, shortages, prices, and safety
      warnings were checked exactly against the cited source when present.
- [ ] Every summary was compared with its cited page.
- [ ] Names, titles, dates, currencies, percentages, and statistics are correct.
- [ ] Allegations are clearly attributed and not presented as proven facts.
- [ ] Court, election, health, finance, crime, and emergency stories received
      extra review.
- [ ] Opinion/editorial content is identified rather than presented as news.
- [ ] Uncertain or OCR-damaged content was removed or corrected.

## Publication

- [ ] All stories marked approved were checked by a human.
- [ ] The edition overview accurately reflects the approved stories.
- [ ] The page includes an AI-summary notice and correction contact/process.
- [ ] Corrections can be made without waiting for another AI run.
